Documentation and guides

The Janeway documentation covers everything you need to set up, run and publish with Janeway, from configuring a press and journal to managing submissions, peer review, production and publication. Guides are organised by role, so editors, authors, reviewers and press managers can go straight to the tasks that are relevant to them.

Browse the Janeway documentation.

Getting help

If you can't find the answer you need in the documentation, there are two ways to get in touch. For account-specific questions, technical problems or anything that involves your journal's data, email the support team. For general questions, tips and conversation with other Janeway users and the development team, join the Janeway community on Discord using this invitation link. When you report a problem, include your journal name, a description of what you were trying to do and any error messages you saw, so that we can help you more quickly.

Training

If you're new to Janeway, we recommend starting with our guides, which introduce the core workflows and terminology you'll use day to day. Our training videos walk through common tasks step by step and are a useful companion to the written guides. For information about live training sessions, onboarding for new journals and other training resources, see our training page.
