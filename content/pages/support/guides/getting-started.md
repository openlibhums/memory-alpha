# Getting started with Janeway

This page takes you through your first steps on a Janeway journal: registering an account, signing in, and any next steps. Each section links to a more detailed guide.

## Creating or finding your Janeway account

Most tasks in Janeway require an account. To create one, click **Register** on the journal website and follow the steps in [Creating an account on Janeway](./creating-an-account-on-janeway.md).

You may not need to register if:

- You are an author submitting an article. An account will be created as part of the submission process.
- You are an reviewer with a journal that uses one-click peer review. The review link in the email may lead dirctly to the review, in this case you do not need to create an account first.
- If you already hold an account for another journal on the press. You can login in with your existing email address and password, but an editor may need to give you the correct permissions for the journal.

If you can't sign in, see [Account troubleshooting](./account-troubleshooting.md) for more information.

## Finding your tasks

The dashboard is where you find tasks and articles assigned to you. What you see in Janeway depends on your roles. You can hold more than one role and the dashboard will show a section for each one. To learn more about navigating Janeway and finding the dashboard, see [Navigating Janeway](./navigating-janeway.md).

Find your role below to see where your tasks appear and which guide to read.

- Author  
   Submit an article through the journal website, and follow it through review, copyediting and proofreading. See the [Author workflow guide](./author-guide.md).
- Reviewer  
   Open a review from the link in your invitation email, or click **View requests** on your dashboard. See the [Reviewer workflow guide](./reviewer-guide.md).
- Copyeditor  
   Open your tasks from the link in your assignment email, or from the **Copyediting requests** page. See the [Copyeditor workflow guide](./copyeditor-guide.md).
- Typesetter  
   Open the task fro the link in the assignment email or select the typesetting block on your dashboard to open the **Typesetting assignments** page. See the [Typesetter workflow guide](./typesetter-guide.md).
- Editor or section editor  
   Manage articles from the workflow stages on your dashboard and in the sidebar. Section editors see only the articles assigned to them. See the [Editor guide overview](./editor-guide-overview.md).
- Journal manager or press manager  
   Configure the journal from the Manager dashboard, or the whole press from the Press manager. See [Setting up your journal](./journal-setup.md).

If you need a role you do not have yet to access a task (copyeditor, section editor, editor), ask a journal manager or editor to assign it.

## Set up your profile

Your profile holds details such as your name, email address, affiliation, ORCID iD, review interests and timezone. To update it, see [Editing your account](./creating-an-account-on-janeway.md#editing-your-account).
