# Getting started with Janeway

This page takes you through your first steps on a Janeway journal: registering an account, signing in, and any next steps. Each section links to a more detailed guide.

## Creating or finding your Janeway account

Most tasks in Janeway require an account. To create one, click **Register** on the journal website and follow the steps in [Creating an account on Janeway](./creating-an-account-on-janeway.md).

You may not need to register if:

- You are an author submitting an article. An account will be created as part of the submission process.
- You are an reviewer with a journal that uses one-click peer review. The review link in the email may lead dirctly to the review, in this case you do not need to create an account first.
- If you already hold an account for another journal on the press. You can login in with your existing email address and password, but an editor may need to give you the correct permissions for the journal.

If you can't sign in, see [Account troubleshooting](./account-troubleshooting.md) for more information.

## Finding your tasks

The dashboard is where you find tasks and articles assigned to you. What you see in Janeway depends on your roles. You can hold more than one role and the dashboard will show a section for each one. To learn more about navigating Janeway and finding the dashboard, see [Navigating Janeway](./navigating-janeway.md).
