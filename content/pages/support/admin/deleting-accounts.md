# Deleting an account

You can delete a user's account in the admin area, but in most cases you shouldn't. Deleting an account removes the link between the user and their work, and it **cannot** be undone. In most scenarios, an account merge or deactivation will be more appropriate.

Choose the option that fits the situation:

- The user no longer needs access  
  Deactivate the account. The user can't sign in, but their record and its links to articles, reviews and editorial work stay intact. You can reactivate the account later. See [Activating accounts](../accounts-and-roles/activating-accounts.md).

- The user has more than one account  
  Always merge duplicate accounts. Merging moves the work from one account to the other, so nothing is lost. See [Merging users](../press-management/managing-users-at-press-level.md#merging-users).

- The user asks for their data to be removed  
  Follow your press's policy. Janeway doesn't set policy for presses, for example on handling data-removal requests. If your policy requires deletion, use the steps below.

## The consequences of deleting an account

When you delete an account, Janeway also deletes records that only exist for that user, including:

- Their roles on every journal and repository
- Their editorial team and staff group memberships
- Their editor assignments on articles
- Their links to articles as an author
- Their affiliations, password reset tokens and contact person entries

Other records stay, but lose their link to the user. For example:

- Review assignments stay, with no reviewer
- Articles stay, with no owner or correspondence author
- Frozen author records stay, so published author names don't change
- Files, notes and log entries stay, with no owner or actor

Janeway lists every record that will be deleted before you confirm. Read the list carefully. If you are unsure, contact your system administrator.

## Delete an account

> [!WARNING]
> Deleting an account can't be undone. Deactivate or merge the account instead, unless your press's policy requires deletion.

1. Open the admin area. See [Navigating admin](./using-admin.md#navigating-admin).
2. Under **Core**, select **Accounts**.
3. Search for the user and open their account.
4. Select **Delete**.
5. Read the list of records that will be deleted.
6. To continue, select **Yes, I'm sure**.
